Braindance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Braindance is an open-source app for finding games to play, for Android, iOS and the web. This page explains what information Braindance handles, why, where it goes, and how long it is kept. If you have a question, email support@braindancegg.com.

Who runs Braindance

Braindance is developed and run by Vladislav Kochetov, who is responsible for the information described on this page. You can reach him at support@braindancegg.com.

The short version

  • You can browse games without an account.
  • Braindance shows no ads, does not sell or share your information for advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • If you sign in, Braindance keeps your name, profile picture, email address and the devices you are signed in on, so it can sign you in and show your account. You can delete your account at any time.

Browsing without an account

To show games, the apps ask the Braindance server for them. Like any website, the server sees your IP address and the request, such as which game you opened.

  • Your IP address is used to deliver the response and to limit how fast one device can send requests, which protects the service from abuse. Braindance does not store it with your requests.
  • Our hosting provider keeps technical request logs, such as the time, address requested and browser or app version, for up to 30 days to run and troubleshoot the service. These logs do not contain your IP address.
  • Game information comes from IGDB and giveaways from GamerPower. The Braindance server asks them for that data itself, so they do not receive your information from Braindance.

Signing in

Sign-in is being added to the apps. You will sign in with Google or Apple; Braindance never sees your password. When you sign in, Braindance receives and stores:

  • Your account ID at Google or Apple, which tells Braindance it is you next time.
  • Your email address and whether the provider has verified it. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, Braindance gets a private relay address instead of your own.
  • Your name and profile picture, if the provider shares them. Apple shares your name only the first time you sign in.

For each device or browser you sign in on, Braindance keeps a sign-in session: the device name your app reports or your browser's name and version, an approximate network location (only the first part of your IP address, which identifies a network rather than a device), and when it was created and last used. You can see and end these sessions from your account. Braindance also keeps a short security log of sign-ins, sign-outs and account changes, with the same approximate network and device details, to protect your account.

Braindance uses this information only to sign you in, show your account, and keep it secure. It never links two sign-in accounts because they share an email address; you can link them yourself while signed in.

On the web, Braindance keeps you signed in with a secure cookie that only the Braindance server can read. The web app also remembers which screen you were on in your browser's storage, which never leaves your device.

Crash reports

If the Android or iOS app crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so the problem can be found and fixed. A report includes what went wrong in the app's code, your device model, operating system version, the app version, and a random identifier for the app installation. It does not include your account or what you were browsing. Crash reports are kept for the limited time Firebase sets for them.

Trailers

Game trailers play in YouTube's embedded player. When you play one, YouTube, a Google service, receives information about your device and network under Google's Privacy Policy. Braindance uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube does not store cookies before you play a trailer.

This website

braindancegg.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive your IP address when you visit. The web app at app.braindancegg.com and the Braindance server are delivered through Cloudflare, which protects them from attacks and also processes your IP address. Braindance does not use analytics or advertising cookies on these sites.

Who handles your information

Braindance uses these services to run. They process information only to provide their service:

  • Google Cloud runs the Braindance server, in the United States.
  • Neon stores account and session data in a database hosted by Amazon Web Services in the United States.
  • Upstash holds short-lived sign-in checks and rate limits, with IP addresses stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, for minutes, on Amazon Web Services in the United States.
  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the web app and the server.
  • Google and Apple handle signing in, and Firebase Crashlytics handles crash reports.

Braindance does not sell your information or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long information is kept

  • Your account, sign-in accounts, sessions and security log are kept until you delete your account. A session stops working after 30 days without use and at most 60 days after you sign in.
  • Request logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Sign-in checks and rate limits expire within minutes.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app once sign-in is available there, or at any time by emailing support@braindancegg.com from the address on your account. Deleting it immediately removes your name, profile picture, email addresses, linked sign-in accounts and sessions, and signs you out everywhere. Braindance keeps only an anonymous record that an account existed and when security events happened, without any network or device details. If you sign in again later, you start a new account.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete the information Braindance has about you by emailing support@braindancegg.com. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. Braindance will answer within 30 days.

Braindance keeps information secure with encrypted connections, stores sign-in tokens only in a form that cannot be turned back into the token, and limits who can access its systems.

Children

Braindance is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and it will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Changes that affect how your information is used are announced on this page before they take effect.
