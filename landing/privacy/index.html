Browsing and searching

Braindance uses your IP address to respond to requests and limit abuse. Hosting and security providers may log request details such as IP addresses, requested URLs and browser information to help run and protect the service. The server and account database are in the US.

When you search, Braindance may send the words you type to IGDB and reuse the results for the same search. Loading game art sends your IP address and the image address to the image host. Opening a giveaway takes you to the provider’s site, where its privacy policy applies.

Your browser may contact Google to load a font. The web app stores your current screen for the tab and your sidebar and theme preferences for future visits, and the Android and iOS apps keep your theme choice on your device. You can clear these in your browser or device settings. See the services Braindance uses.

Watching trailers

Braindance uses YouTube API Services for trailers. Loading a thumbnail sends Google your IP address and the image URL. The embedded player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube may process device and playback data, use cookies or similar storage, and show ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Accounts

If you sign in, Google or Apple shares your account ID, email address, verification status and any name or profile picture it provides. With Apple’s Hide My Email, Braindance receives a relay address.

Braindance uses session records, device or browser details and part of your IP address to keep you signed in and protect your account. Web sign-in uses a secure cookie. Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity or 60 days from sign-in, whichever comes first.

If you set up an authenticator or passkey, Braindance keeps the encrypted authenticator key, hashed recovery codes or public passkey details until you remove the method or delete the account. Braindance may send a security email after repeated failed attempts. Your fingerprint, face or PIN stays on your device.

Delete your account

Email support@braindancegg.com with your request. Use a linked address if you can. If you can’t, tell me so I can verify the request another way.

Deletion signs you out and removes your profile, email addresses, linked sign-ins, sessions and verification methods from the active database. Braindance keeps an internal account ID, creation and deletion dates, and security events with their times and, where recorded, the provider or session reference. It clears device and network details from those events. Support messages, logs and crash reports are handled separately.

Mobile crashes and test invitations

In mobile builds with crash reporting enabled, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash details, device model, app and system versions, and installation IDs so I can fix problems. Google begins deleting these records after 90 days.

If you accept a test invitation, Firebase uses tester names, emails and device identifiers to deliver the app and collects any feedback you send. iOS tests may also require registering your device with Apple. Firebase keeps tester information until deletion is requested and removes it within 180 days of the request.

Contact and privacy rights

Email support@braindancegg.com to access, correct, cancel or object to the use of your information (ARCO rights in Mexico). You can also request a copy, ask to limit use or disclosure, or withdraw consent where it applies. Include your name, how to reach you and what you’re requesting. I’ll explain how to verify your identity or a representative’s authority. Please don’t send passwords or recovery codes.

I use support messages, attachments and your email address to handle requests and keep a record of the response. I may disclose information when required by law. For Mexican ARCO requests, I’ll give you a decision within 20 business days and carry out an approved request within another 15 business days. A justified extension is possible once per period, for the same length of time. If you disagree with my decision or receive no response, you can contact Mexico’s Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.

Braindance is for ages 13 and up. If a child under 13 has shared personal information, email me so I can investigate and remove it where appropriate. I’ll announce material changes here before they take effect.
